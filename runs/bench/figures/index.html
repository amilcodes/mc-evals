Piston Door Bench · results as of 15 Nov 2026

Agents designing Minecraft piston doors, scored by how small the door is

Each run gives one model a time limit and a door size. The agent builds a seamless piston door, tests it in the real game engine and tries to shrink it. The score is the volume of the door's bounding box: smaller is better, and a door that fails the test scores nothing.

    Source: Piston Door Bench, waves 1 to 8, 58 finished runs. Every door was re-tested in a fresh simulator, through eight open-and-close cycles at varied timing, before it counted. Volume is width × height × depth of the smallest box holding every block the door uses at any moment, the doorway included and the lever's cell left out. Runs per cell are small (one to four), so neighbouring results are not statistically settled.
