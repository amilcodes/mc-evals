# Wave 8: Opus 3x3 seamless, standard vs changed physics

Changed physics: repeater step 3 ticks, observer delay 3 ticks (standard: 2 and 2). Kit 1,
120 minutes, same launch prompt and continue rule as waves 1-7. All 13 runs below passed the
final (strict) grade; volumes are final volumes, lower is better.

| | standard physics | changed physics |
|---|---|---|
| wave 8 (launched together) | 264, 360, 520 | 70, 112, 264 |
| earlier waves | 80, 154, 216, 312 (w1, w3) | 112, 112, 216 (w5) |
| pooled median | 264 (n=7) | 112 (n=6) |
| pooled geometric mean | 236 | 133 |

Standard / changed geometric-mean ratio: 1.77 pooled (one-sided permutation p = 0.05),
2.88 within wave 8 (p = 0.10, n = 3 vs 3). Wave 8 cost $117 API-equivalent for 6 runs.

Reading: changed physics did not hurt Opus; if anything its doors were smaller. Opus's
standard-physics doors (80-520) are not recalled community designs either, so at this
level the model designs from the rules in TASK.md in both worlds, and the physics change
does not separate recall from reasoning. Possible reasons the changed-physics doors came
out smaller: the brief's "designs that work in standard Minecraft may not work here"
line may push more lab testing before building; small samples and large run-to-run
spread (70-264 vs 80-520) also leave room for chance.
